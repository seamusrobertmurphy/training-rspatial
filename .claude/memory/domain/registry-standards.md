# Registry standards

Verified against the primary documents in `references/standards/`. Dated at the moment of checking.

- 2026-09-03: ACR IFM on Non-Federal U.S. Forestlands v2.1 (July 2024) s.7.5 verified. Equation 22 total uncertainty includes the HWP terms; Equation 23 is zero at or below 10 per cent and UNCt minus 10 per cent above it; precision requirement is plus or minus 10 per cent at 90 per cent confidence. The book's `pmax(0, u - 10)` is correct.
- 2026-09-03: VMD0055 v1.1 (21 October 2024) verified. Equations 10 and 11 give the carbon stock change discount factor, zero at or below 10 per cent then U% x 0.4307 / (100 x 1.6449). The separate area inflation factor for unplanned deforestation is Equation 29. FCBM accuracy thresholds are 70 per cent for deforestation over the HRP, above 90 for forest at its end, relaxing to 60 and 80 where at least half the jurisdiction's forest carries under 50 per cent canopy cover.
- 2026-09-03: **ART TREES 3.0 was approved by the ART Board in June 2026 and released 25 June 2026.** The constants 0.524417 and 1.645006 are unchanged from TREES 2.0 and Equation 1 remains the crediting level, but the uncertainty adjustment factor moved from Equation 11 in 2.0 to **Equations 7 and 8** in 3.0, split between emission reductions and removals. The book's uncertainty chapter and the CLAUDE.md verified-constants table both still cite Equation 11 and both need updating. The copy in `references/standards/ART/ART-3.0/` is only the July 2025 public comment draft; download the approved version.
- 2026-09-03: ACR Framework for Remotely Sensed Quantification of Forest Carbon is v1.0 dated 27 March 2026, which matches the book's citation, but the document is absent from `references/standards/` so its two thresholds (RMSE uncertainty below 20 per cent, 90 per cent CI uncertainty at or below 10 per cent) are unverified against the source.
