- 2026-09-06: the IPCC natural disturbance disaggregation guidance is the 2019 Refinement, Volume 4, Chapter 2, Section 2.6, in `references/standards/IPCC-md`. Box 2.2k, after EU Regulation 2018/841, builds a wildfire emissions series from 1971 (base year minus twenty), iteratively strips values above the mean plus two standard deviations until the distribution is normal, and calls the surviving mean the background level and twice its standard deviation the margin. - 2026-09-06: the recorded constant that Lake Chilwa's endorheic sink cannot survive hydrological conditioning is wrong and is corrected. Running all five WhiteboxTools conditioning algorithms on the same grid shows the `max_depth` cap is what destroys the basin, not conditioning as such. Capped at 3 m the breach cannot carve an outlet from a 400 m basin so it fills instead, raising the lake bed 14.1 m on average and 83.5 m at most.
